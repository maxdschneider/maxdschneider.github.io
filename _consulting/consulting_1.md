I work with the Italian National Institute of Geophysics and Volcanology to improve how earthquake forecasts are communicated and used in emergency management. I am developing maps and visualizations for earthquake forecast reports that are sent to Italy’s Department of Civil Protection, incorporating the needs and concerns of stakeholders within Italy’s [complex sociocultural setting for earthquake forecasting](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212420926003122). These new products will be evaluated with key stakeholders to ensure that the forecast report can support decisions in earthquake response and emergency management at national, regional, and local levels.
