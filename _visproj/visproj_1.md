Aftershock forecasts are used to make critical post-earthquake decisions by numerous professions, including emergency managers, engineers, public health officials and risk communicators. I am leading a multi-agency effort across the US, Mexico, El Salvador and New Zealand to develop new maps, graphics and interactive software for communicating aftershock forecasts to meet the varied needs of these diverse user groups. We elicited the needs for aftershock forecast products from 12 professions in three countries, using a novel method for stakeholder engagement. We then developed 100+ new maps and graphics for aftershock forecasts centered on these needs, systematizing the process of designing such products into a new technique for user-centered design. We further developed interactive software that allows users to design their own aftershock forecast maps based on their specific requirements. These product innovations have already been to support urban search and rescue (among other uses) following recent earthquakes in Turkey, Morocco, and El Salvador.
