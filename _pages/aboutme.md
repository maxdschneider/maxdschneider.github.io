<div markdown="1">
I am fascinated by how society addresses gargantuan risks on our planet using predictive models, forecasting, and decision support tools. In my 15+ years leading interdisciplinary research groups, I have advanced the science and communication of forecasting for earthquakes, hurricanes and climate change, improving decision-making for crisis response and preparedness. I combine my deep statistical arsenal with understanding complex hazards data and user specifications to develop models and carefully quantify uncertainty and risk. I also translate these risk metrics to make them relevant for stakeholders, designing visual products and software that can meet actual user needs. By weaving together training from the statistical, physical and social sciences, my work has shaped forecasting operations and tools to communicate risk for science agencies across the globe. My [ resume](https://maxdschneider.github.io/assets/pdf/Schneider-Resume.pdf) has more details on my multidisciplinary training and experience.

<br>

In my free time, I bike the hills of San Francisco, drum in a [Brazilian music ensemble](https://www.locoblocosf.org/), and [take photos of what strikes me](https://maxdschneider.github.io/photos/).
